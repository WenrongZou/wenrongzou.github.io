From September 2026, I am a Ph.D. student at the Institut Camille Jordan, Université Jean Monnet Saint-Étienne, working with Filippo A. E. Nuccio and Riccardo Brasca. My Ph.D. is part of the ANR project FALSE (Formalising Arithmetic in the Lean Software Environment).

I am interested in the formalisation of mathematics in the Lean proof assistant, especially mathematics related to number theory. More on the research page.

Before that, I completed my M.Sc. in Mathematics at the University of Bonn under the supervision of María Inés de Frutos Fernández and Floris van Doorn, and obtained my bachelor's degree at Sichuan University.
