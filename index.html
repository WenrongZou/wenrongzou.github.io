About

I have just completed my M.Sc. in Mathematics at the University of Bonn, where I was supervised by María Inés de Frutos Fernández and Floris van Doorn. From September 2026 I am a Ph.D. student of Filippo Nuccio and Riccardo Brasca at the Institut Camille Jordan, Université Jean Monnet Saint-Étienne. Before Bonn I obtained my bachelor's degree at Sichuan University.

My Ph.D. is part of the ANR project FALSE (Formalising Arithmetic in the Lean Software Environment).

Research

I am interested in the formalisation of mathematics in the Lean proof assistant, especially mathematics related to number theory.

Interests

  • Formalisation of mathematics in Lean / Mathlib
  • Algebraic number theory

Formalisation projects

  • Formal Group Laws Formalisation of formal group laws in Lean.
  • Robo A game for learning Lean 4, where a smart-elf joins you on your exploration of the Leaniverse. Mentor: Marcus Zibrowius. Work carried out as an AI for Math Summer Fellow.
